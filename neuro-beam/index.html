          Model definition & integrity

          transparent, reproducible, non-causal
          τm dV/dt = −(V−EL) + Rm(Iinj − w + Ih)

          Adaptive leaky integrate-and-fire membrane equation with an Ih-like sag contribution.

          dw/dt = −w/τw ; spike ⇒ w ← w + b

          Adaptation term controls spike-frequency adaptation. Gene-specific shifts are derived from observed Patch-seq correlations.

          Model boundary: “higher-expression-like” and “lower-expression-like” are phenotype-conditioned simulations constructed from observational correlation fingerprints. They are not experimental gene knock-in/knock-out predictions and should be used for hypothesis generation and model comparison.

          How to interpret the signal

          research-integrity layer
          Signed score = (mutation % / 100) × Spearman ρ

          Preserves whether neuronal expression of the selected gene is positively or negatively associated with the chosen electrical phenotype.

          Absolute score = (mutation % / 100) × |Spearman ρ|

          Useful for prioritizing the magnitude of cross-domain overlap without interpreting the sign as a disease effect.

          Critical interpretation: the electrical association comes from mouse visual-cortex Patch-seq neurons, while the mutation prevalence comes from human cancer cohorts. Their integration is a reproducible hypothesis-generation framework, not evidence that a cancer mutation causes that neuronal electrical phenotype.
          ABOUT

          About the Platform

          Research infrastructure for bioelectric, molecular and disease-genomics discovery.

          GDRN NETWORK · BIO-IT RESEARCH INFRASTRUCTURE

          NEURO-BEAM integrates neuronal bioelectric phenotypes with molecular signatures and comparative disease genomics in a single research environment.

          Designed for scientists working across computational neuroscience, genomics, cancer biology, electrophysiology and translational bioinformatics, the platform enables researchers to move from a measured electrical phenotype to associated genes, disease-cohort alterations and analysis-ready numerical outputs. Its central value is the unification of electrophysiological evidence and molecular disease data within one queryable framework, allowing precise hypothesis generation, cross-cohort comparison and exportable datasets for independent downstream analysis.

          Platform benchmark

          NEURO-BEAM follows the research-portal principles established by mature bioinformatics systems: interactive cohort exploration, gene-centric querying, transparent numerical evidence, analysis-ready exports and separation between scientific user experience and computational infrastructure.

          Advanced statistical framework

          Inference is donor-aware, effect-size focused and externally validated.

          Unsupervised discovery

          Robust scaling, PCA, GMM, HDBSCAN, hierarchical and consensus clustering.

          Cluster evidence

          Silhouette, Calinski–Harabasz, Davies–Bouldin, bootstrap stability and donor stability.

          Gene coupling

          Mixed-effects models, pseudobulk confirmation, BH-FDR, effect size and 95% CI.

          Validation

          Donor-level splits, untouched external motor-cortex cohort, calibration and reproducibility checks.

          Explainable ML

          Elastic Net, RF, XGBoost, SVM and later pathway-aware multimodal NeuroBEAM-Net.

          Research integrity

          No causal language from association alone; no UMAP-defined clusters; no donor leakage.
