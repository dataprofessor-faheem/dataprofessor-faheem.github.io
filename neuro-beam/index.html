03

Advanced statistical framework

Inference is donor-aware, effect-size focused and externally validated.

Unsupervised discovery

Robust scaling, PCA, GMM, HDBSCAN, hierarchical and consensus clustering.

Cluster evidence

Silhouette, Calinski–Harabasz, Davies–Bouldin, bootstrap stability and donor stability.

Gene coupling

Mixed-effects models, pseudobulk confirmation, BH-FDR, effect size and 95% CI.

Validation

Donor-level splits, untouched external motor-cortex cohort, calibration and reproducibility checks.

Explainable ML

Elastic Net, RF, XGBoost, SVM and later pathway-aware multimodal NeuroBEAM-Net.

Research integrity

No causal language from association alone; no UMAP-defined clusters; no donor leakage.
