VERIFIED ACCESS MODEL

Free access for genuine students and researchers.

Users register first. Their account remains pending until an administrator verifies their academic or scientific identity. Approved users can save figure projects.

1

Register

Email, name, institution, discipline and account type.

2

Provide scientific identity

Student ID reference, ORCID, institutional profile or another verifiable scientific identifier.

3

Admin review

The platform administrator approves, rejects or suspends access.

4

Create

Approved users save projects and use the publication studio.
