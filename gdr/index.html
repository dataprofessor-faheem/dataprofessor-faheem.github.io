The GDR proposition

A global research platform built around unanswered questions — not disciplinary boundaries.

Important discoveries increasingly require complementary expertise that no single laboratory or discipline can provide alone.

GDR is designed to transform a scientifically defensible research gap into a multidisciplinary Discovery Challenge. The platform maps the specialists, methods, datasets, technologies and validation capabilities required, then brings them together in a structured international research program.

Question-firstThe problem determines the team and methodology.
Evidence-ledNovelty is assessed against existing knowledge before a public call.
Contribution-basedScientific roles, responsibilities and outputs reflect actual work.

Discovery network architecture

Connect the right specialist to the right scientific problem.

Every project should deliberately combine complementary scientific capabilities rather than assemble collaborators without a defined methodological role.

01 • GAP

Scientific Gap Mapping

Classify what is unknown, contradictory, under-tested, geographically limited, technologically constrained or not independently validated.

02 • EXPERTISE

Multidisciplinary Expertise Map

Identify required disciplines, techniques, infrastructure, computational methods, datasets and experimental capabilities.

03 • MATCH

International Collaborator Recruitment

Advertise defined scientific roles and evaluate researchers according to specialization, methodology and proposed contribution.

04 • PROTOCOL

Structured Research Development

Move from hypothesis to protocol, ethics, data governance, statistical analysis plan and reproducibility framework.

05 • VALIDATE

Cross-Disciplinary Validation

Integrate computational, experimental, clinical, field or molecular evidence as appropriate to the research question.

06 • IMPACT

Outputs & Translation

Prepare reproducible manuscripts, datasets, software, methods, patents or translational outputs after appropriate scientific review.
