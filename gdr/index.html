The GDR proposition

Research begins with an important question, then finds the right expertise.

GDR is designed as a discovery network rather than an ordinary researcher directory or journal portal.

Researchers can contribute ideas, identify unresolved gaps, participate in structured multidisciplinary challenges, and join teams according to their actual scientific specialization, methodology and potential contribution.

Question-firstThe scientific problem determines the team.
Evidence-ledNovelty must be screened against existing knowledge.
Contribution-basedRoles and outputs reflect real scientific work.
