6 · Source provenance and reporting integrity

The historical aggregate data are openly inspectable in benchmark-data.json. The source workbook contains restricted study-derived records and third-party database material, which have not been publicly redistributed on this site.

SwissADME, ProTox 3.0, and ADMETlab 3.0 have different endpoints and tasks. No directly comparable superiority claim is made. Future independent predictive validation will require recovered trained models, externally sourced compound labels, overlap checks, and a locked evaluation protocol.
