METHODS AND TRANSPARENCY

Designed for reproducible research

01. Chemical curation

Parse and canonicalize single-component organic SMILES; flag duplicates and invalid records without inventing labels.

02. Molecular descriptors

Formula, exact software version, molecular weight, logP, TPSA, hydrogen bonding, QED and rule-of-five screening.

03. Structural analytics

Radius-2 Morgan fingerprints, Tanimoto similarity, scaffold diversity, Butina clustering and two-dimensional PCA.

04. Auditable reports

SHA-256 analysis fingerprint, structured JSON/CSV tables, computational summaries and downloadable PDF documentation.
