The JBR proposition

Research should begin with the best question — then assemble the right expertise.

Scientific expertise is often distributed across laboratories, disciplines and countries. JBR is being built to bridge those boundaries.

Instead of functioning as another journal portal or generic researcher directory, JBR begins with a defensible research gap. The platform then structures the problem, maps the expertise needed, advertises a multidisciplinary collaboration opportunity, and supports the team from protocol to validated output.

Question-firstThe scientific problem determines the team.
Evidence-ledNovelty is screened against existing knowledge.
Contribution-basedRoles and outputs should reflect real scientific work.
