Why JBR

A research network built around questions, not silos.

Many important scientific problems require expertise that is distributed across different institutions and disciplines. JBR is designed to convert a strong research idea into a structured discovery challenge, identify the missing expertise, and assemble a team capable of addressing the question rigorously.

The platform is broader than health analytics: it is intended for health informatics, AI, biotechnology, bioinformatics, drug discovery, plant science, zoology, microbiology, biochemistry, molecular biology, environmental science and related quantitative disciplines.
