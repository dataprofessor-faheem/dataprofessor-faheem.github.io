Decision support, not black-box prediction

A national crop-weather intelligence layer for Saudi agriculture.

NABAT combines region, crop, live climate, forecast conditions and transparent risk rules. Every output identifies the forecast period, dominant drivers and confidence so users can understand why the score changed.

Reading the output

What does a NABAT risk value mean?

0–24LOW

No strong climate hazard signal. Continue routine monitoring.

25–49MODERATE

One or more conditions are becoming favourable for stress or disease. Increase observation.

50–74HIGH

Meaningful environmental hazard window. Prioritise field scouting and management review.

75–100CRITICAL

Strong combined climate signal. Rapid field assessment and expert agronomic decision are advised.

Important: NABAT reports environmental suitability and crop stress—not diagnosis, infection prevalence, yield loss or pesticide need. Operational recommendations must be validated against local field observations and Saudi-approved agronomic guidance.

Methodological status

Transparent now. Calibrated next.

The current NABAT engine is deliberately interpretable. It uses explicit crop-specific climate thresholds and forecast signals. Future model versions should be calibrated against Saudi field-confirmed disease, pest, phenology and yield observations using spatial and temporal external validation.

  • Sentinel-2 / Landsat vegetation and thermal indicators

  • Farm IoT soil, canopy and microclimate sensors
  • Field-confirmed disease and pest observations

About NABAT

A GDR Network agricultural bio-IT initiative.

NABAT is designed as an extensible research and decision-support platform connecting agrometeorology, crop science, plant pathology, remote sensing, machine learning and farm-level observation. The objective is scientifically traceable forecasting that can be evaluated, improved and validated—not opaque automated advice.
