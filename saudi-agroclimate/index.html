Scientific framework

Methods

The operational browser engine is an interpretable baseline that exposes its input variables and risk logic. Production disease models will be promoted only after Saudi field labels support independent spatial and temporal validation, probability calibration and decision-threshold analysis.

VALIDATION ROADMAP
  • Official NCM station/gridded weather and agricultural parameters
  • Field polygons and Sentinel-2 time-series features
  • Crop growth stage, cultivar and management context
  • Field-confirmed disease/pest labels and severity
  • Spatial hold-out validation by farm/region
  • Temporal hold-out validation by season/year
  • Discrimination, calibration, Brier score and decision utility

Platform

About

NABAT brings agrometeorology, crop science, plant pathology, remote sensing, geospatial analytics and reproducible modelling into one decision-support environment. The goal is not automation for its own sake; it is better-timed human decisions supported by evidence that can be inspected and improved.
