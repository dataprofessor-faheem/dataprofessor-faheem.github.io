From sequence data to reproducible evidence.

An open modular workbench for scientific sequence quality control, alignment-aware conservation analysis and future vaccine informatics integrations. This demonstrator does not predict vaccine efficacy or generate validated epitopes.

Sequence workbench

Paste protein FASTA or select a local file. The text stays in your browser. For conservation, upload a previously aligned, equal-length protein MSA; equal lengths alone do not establish a valid biological alignment.

Versioned configurations, supplementary outputs and reproducibility manifest.

GDRN-VaxForge is experimental scientific software, not a clinical device. Biological validity, safety, immune protection and clinical benefit cannot be established by this demonstration. External predictors and services are not yet connected. Original paper foundation: Vaccines (2023), 11, 1090.
